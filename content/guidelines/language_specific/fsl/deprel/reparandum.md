The rel `reparandum` refers to the relation between one element and its repair. It involves mainly cases of disfluency. 

<conll>
# sent_id = Alpha_entretien_etudiant_souvenir__2
# text = [CLASSE] [CLASSE] [GROUPE] [CHAUD]
# source_file = ./Souvenir_JAN_1.mp4
# text_fr = Dans la classe, le groupe était chaleureux,
# text_en = In the class, the group was warm,
1	[CLASSE]	[CLASSE]	NOUN	_	_	2	reparandum	_	AlignBegin=5570|AlignEnd=6103|Gloss=[CLASS]
2	[CLASSE]	[CLASSE]	NOUN	_	_	4	mod	_	AlignBegin=6103|AlignEnd=6709|Gloss=[CLASS]|NMM=eyebrow-raise+eye-blink
3	[GROUPE]	[GROUPE]	NOUN	_	_	4	subj	_	AlignBegin=6715|AlignEnd=7483|Gloss=[GROUP]|NMM=eye-contact+forward-tilt
4	[CHAUD]	[CHAUD]	ADJ	_	_	0	root	_	AlignBegin=7494|AlignEnd=8395|Gloss=[WARM]|NMM=eyebrow-frown+eye-blink+forward-tilt
</conll>

In this example, the first sign is repeated because the signer is still elaborating the sentence to come. On the second sign, we can see that she makes eye contact with the other signer, and produces the sentence without other disfluencies.
